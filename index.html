I know I wasn't perfect, and there are things I wish I had handled differently. I have things to learn, and I want to carry those lessons with me as I continue growing into a better person.

Mamamia, I hope life gives you so many reasons to smile. I hope you reach the dreams you've been working toward, discover new things that make you happy, and find courage whenever life gets difficult. When things become overwhelming, I hope you remember that you are capable of getting through more than you sometimes realize. You deserve kindness, peace, genuine happiness, and people who appreciate you for who you are.

I may not be beside you the way I once was, but that doesn't erase the care and gratitude I have for you. I will always wish you well, cheer for your achievements from wherever life takes me, and hope that you're taking care of yourself. Ingat ka palagi, ha? No matter where life takes us, I will always be grateful that I got to know you, love you, and share a part of my life with you. The memories we made will remain a meaningful part of my story. I don't need to pretend that they meant nothing just because things have changed. They mattered to me, and so did you.

And if there's one thing I hope you carry with you on your birthday, it's this: never doubt that you are someone worth celebrating. You don't have to be perfect, have everything figured out, or make everyone happy to deserve good things. I hope you continue becoming the person you want to be, at your own pace, and that you find happiness in the little things along the way.

To my once Grow Old With You, thank you for the laughter, the lessons, the memories, and the moments that became special simply because I shared them with you. I'll always be thankful that our paths crossed. Whatever the future holds, I sincerely hope it treats you gently and brings you the happiness you deserve.

Happy birthday once again, Mamamia. I hope your day is filled with love, laughter, warmth, and all the little things that make your heart happy. Mahal na mahal kita, and I will always be grateful for the part you played in my life. Thank you for being you. Until our paths cross again. 🤍
